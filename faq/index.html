Getting started

What is 6dgrs?

6dgrs is a social discovery network built around real relationships. It helps you discover people, places and plans through people you know and trust.

Is 6dgrs a dating app?

No. It is designed for social discovery, travel, introductions and real-world connection more broadly.

Who can use 6dgrs?

6dgrs is for people aged 18 or over.

Is 6dgrs free?

For the initial beta, yes. Features and the commercial model may evolve.

Where is 6dgrs available?

6dgrs is currently launched and primarily operated from the United Kingdom. The Service may be accessible elsewhere and feature availability may vary.

Trusted, Met and Vouch

What does Trusted mean?

Trusted is a directional relationship signal you choose for someone you genuinely know and trust. It is intentionally selective, not identity verification or a safety guarantee. You can currently choose up to six outgoing Trusted relationships; there is no 6-to-12 upgrade system.

What’s the difference between Trusted, Met and Vouch?

Trusted is a trust choice. Met is context from a confirmed interaction between two 6dgrs accounts. Vouch is contextual endorsement from an eligible person. They are independent signals, not levels of progression, and none is a background check.

What is a connection path?

A path explains how someone sits within your wider network. It may be partial and does not mean everyone in it knows, recommends or endorses everyone else.

How many degrees can I explore?

The current released discovery experience can surface eligible network context up to three degrees. Six degrees remains the broader concept behind the name as the product evolves.

What is a Personal Key?

A Personal Key enables an intentional lookup or connection request. Entering or scanning one does not itself create a relationship or entitlement to contact someone.

What are introductions?

Introductions add human context to a connection. They do not obligate anyone to respond, meet or continue talking.

Discovering people and connection paths

How do I discover people?

6dgrs uses eligible relationship and network context rather than making every account openly searchable.

Can anyone message me?

No. Visibility does not automatically create permission to message. Direct relationship contexts and approved Met conversations may permit contact, subject to privacy and blocking rules; wider connections may use an Introduction.

Does connection context mean someone is safe?

No. Trusted, Met, Vouch, Personal Key and connection paths are useful context, not guarantees about identity, character or safety.

Trips and Plans

What are Trips?

Trips let you add a destination so 6dgrs can provide relevant network and place context. They are not public live tracking.

What are Plans?

Plans are user-created activities for doing things together through relevant network and location context. Joining a Plan means consent to that activity only.

How does location work?

You intentionally provide selected place or Trip context. Structured places can include coordinates internally for place resolution, while ordinary experiences show authorised broad or city-level context. 6dgrs does not currently collect live device GPS.

Are Plans organised by 6dgrs?

Generally no. Plans are user-created and not supervised or endorsed by 6dgrs. Make your own decision about whether one is appropriate.

Privacy and safety

Who can see my profile?

Visibility depends on your setting, relationship context and the feature being used. Hidden prevents ordinary network discovery; an intentional Personal Key route is separate.

Can people see my exact location?

Ordinary profile and network surfaces use authorised broad or city-level context, not raw coordinates. Selected places may contain coordinates internally for place resolution.

Does 6dgrs verify its users?

6dgrs does not currently offer a consumer identity-verification feature. Trusted, Met and Vouch are social context, not identity verification.

How do I block or report someone?

Use in-app blocking and reporting tools. A block is a boundary and must not be circumvented. Safety concerns can be sent to safety@6dgrs.app.

Is 6dgrs an emergency service?

No. If there is immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services.

Account and support

How do I change my profile?

Use profile and Settings controls in the app. Some information may be subject to account rules.

How do I delete my account?

Account deletion can be requested through the Service with verification or reauthentication. Requests are ordinarily processed within 30 days; see the Privacy Policy for retention details.

How do I contact 6dgrs?

General: hello@6dgrs.app. Safety: safety@6dgrs.app. Privacy: privacy@6dgrs.app. Feedback: feedback@6dgrs.app.

How do I report a technical problem?

Email hello@6dgrs.app or use the feedback route in the Service. Never send passwords or authentication codes.
