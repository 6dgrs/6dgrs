Questions, answered

Good to know.

What is 6dgrs?

6dgrs is a social discovery network built around real relationships. It helps you discover people, places and plans through people you already know and trust.

Is 6dgrs a dating app?

No. 6dgrs is designed for social discovery, travel, introductions and real-world connection more broadly.

What’s the difference between Trusted, Met and Vouch?

Trusted is a relationship choice. Met is context from a confirmed interaction between two 6dgrs accounts. Vouch is contextual endorsement. They are independent signals, not levels of progression or safety guarantees.

How do I discover new people?

6dgrs uses eligible relationship and network context. The current released discovery experience can surface context up to three degrees; six degrees remains the broader concept behind the name.

Can people see my location or find me automatically?

You control important aspects of visibility. Selected places and Trips provide broad context; 6dgrs does not currently collect live device GPS.

Does connection context mean someone is safe?

No. Trusted, Met, Vouch, Personal Keys and connection paths provide context, not background checks or guarantees about identity, character or safety.
